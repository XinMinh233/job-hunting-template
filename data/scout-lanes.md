## HOW THIS FILE WORKS

One section per tier lane. Each section lists: the sources that
actually work for that lane (with notes on which render full JDs vs.
shells), search terms that hit, avoid-patterns, and dated learnings
from real sweeps. The scout follows this file over generic searching —
it is the system's accumulated street knowledge.

## METHOD NOTES (inherited from the original hunt — generic, keep)

- PRIMARY SOURCES BEAT AGGREGATORS. Aggregators (LinkedIn, Himalayas,
  We Work Remotely, RemoteOK, ...) are where you FIND a role, never
  where you learn anything about it. Always click through to the
  employer's own ATS posting (Greenhouse / Ashby / Lever / Workday /
  icims) before believing geography, dates, or deadlines.
- ATS-PLATFORM SWEEPS ARE A CHEAP DETECTOR. Searching Greenhouse /
  Lever / Ashby boards directly for "<your city / country>" + role
  terms surfaces employers hiring in your market that local boards
  miss — in the original hunt this was a near-pure detector for
  foreign employers hiring locally, and it is fully web-reachable.
- HUNT THE LONG TAIL. Famous companies are over-contested. The long
  tail of smaller firms in your niche is more numerous and less
  contested — build lane knowledge about where they post.
- LinkedIn truncates descriptions behind "see more" — follow the
  "Apply on company website" link to the real ATS URL and read that.
- Some markets have app/login-gated boards that web search cannot
  reach (in China: BOSS直聘 / 猎聘 / 拉勾 / 智联). List yours below —
  the scout will remind you to hand-search them instead of pretending
  to cover them.
